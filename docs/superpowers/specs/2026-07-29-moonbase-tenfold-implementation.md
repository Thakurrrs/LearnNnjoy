# LearnNnjoy — Moonbase Tenfold Implemented Story

**Status:** Four-quest connected story implemented
**Grade:** 7
**Concept:** Large-number place value, grouping, comparison, and estimation

## Story promise

Blink, a playful camera drone, follows a rare comet bloom beyond the moonbase
telescope map. Nova and the child follow Blink’s signal, rebuild the enormous
coordinate, transmit it in two grouping systems, and photograph the comet.

The child operates the story world before Nova names the mathematical
relationship.

## Connected quest chain

1. **The Telescope Wakes**
   - Move the telescope through six place-value rings.
   - Watch the same digit represent ten times more at every place to the left.
   - Nova explicitly distinguishes the digit from its changing value.

2. **Rebuild the Coordinate**
   - Bundle ten matching location units into one larger unit.
   - Assemble Blink’s complete coordinate from place-value modules.

3. **Two Mission Controls**
   - Inspect the same coordinate at Delhi Mission Control and the world
     research station.
   - Indian and international comma grouping changes while the location stays
     fixed.

4. **Catch the Comet**
   - Compare two nearby comet-trail coordinates.
   - Choose a useful estimate to the nearest lakh.
   - Use the exact coordinate to focus the telescope and capture the comet
     photograph.

## Ending act

Blink returns and the comet photograph fills the moonbase dome. Nova briefly
connects the completed actions: place created value, grouping named the same
number in two ways, exact coordinates found the comet, and estimation helped
the telescope aim quickly.

## Product constraints preserved

- No timer, danger, leaderboard, or peer comparison.
- No exponents or orbital-physics detour.
- No operation drill inserted only for curriculum breadth.
- Home, device save, journal replay, quest map, and reduced-motion behaviour
  remain available.
